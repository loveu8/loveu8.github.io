Starlux Airlines Co., Ltd is a Taiwanese full-service carrier headquartered in Taipei established in 2018. Starlux Airlines Co., Ltd has established a branch office in Australia (STARLUX) to support its planned passenger and cargo operations in the Australian market, including sales and marketing, customer service, cargo activities and airport-related operations.

STARLUX is looking for a Cargo Manager for the branch office. The position is full-time and based at Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport, Mascot, Sydney.

The tasks and responsibilities of this role include the following:

- Plan, organise, manage and oversee the STARLUX branch office's cargo inventory, warehouse, and ground handling operations Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport;
- Handle STARLUX's cargo sales duties and develop a local cargo network;
- Manage cargo reservations and operations;
- Direct the activities of cargo operations and cargo sales staff;
- Supervise and ensure cargo personnel follows standard operation procedures, workflows and complies with local laws and regulations in terms of cargo imports and exports, loading and unloading;
- Recruit, train, supervise, and manage performance of cargo operations and cargo sales staff;
- Establish, build, and maintain positive relations with local agents and freight forwarders;
- Serve as the liaison between the STARLUX branch office in Australia and the head office in Taiwan's Cargo Division;
- Be responsible for cargo related manpower estimation and budget planning for the branch office;
- Meet cargo sales targets;
- Based on cargo operation irregularity statistics, achieve operations KPIs;

Skills & Experience required for the position

- Bachelor's degree in management, business, or similar;
- At least 5 years' experience in airline cargo operations positions;
- Experience using airline iCargo system;
- Experience with Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW) and IATA CASS system;
- Be proficient with MS Office tools;
- Have excellent multitasking, communication and leadership skills;
- Can work rotating shifts including nights, holidays, weekends and days off.
- Be flexible to work additional hours on short notice when required.
